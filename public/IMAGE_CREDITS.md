## Rendering standards

- Public landing uses `public/site-v5.css` as its single served stylesheet.
- Core site imagery is served locally: four WebP photos and the NASA WebP, plus original SVG layers.
- No external image service is required for the core landing visual; Google Fonts remains a runtime font CDN dependency with browser fallback fonts.
- OS dashboard is labeled **INTERACTIVE CONCEPT**. Market routes, map lines and progress indicators are illustrative, not real authenticated case KPIs.
- Country chips indicate possible destination research, not proof of current operations in those countries.
- No fake customers, revenue, institutional badges or unverifiable partnerships in public visuals.
